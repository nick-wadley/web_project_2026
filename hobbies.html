What Got Nicholas Into the Music and the Gym

There are factors that got Nick into the Music and the Gym. First of all, growing up, his sister listened to music with him almost everyday. The main form of that was via the "Just Dance" series, in which the siblings performed choregraphed dance moves that aligned with a popular song. Nicholas's friends also listened to music in Middle and High School. As far as the gym goes, he was required to take a weight training class in his senior year of high school for the basketball program. Ever since then, he has been dedicated to maintaining his cardiovascular health by going to the gym.

Nicholas's Love for Xbox

Nicholas has played the gaming console known as Xbox since he was a child. His older brother owned one when Nick was still in elementary school, and they would play together for hours on end. This led him to purchasing a console in fifth grade, and he has not looked back. Nicholas favorite games are sports titles like NBA2K (basketball) and Madden NFL (American football). His favorite way to play video games, moreover, is with his friends via voice chat.

Nicholas's Anime-Watching

Instead of watching typical Netflix or reality television shows, Nicholas usually watches the film type known as Anime. Originating from Japan, this type of film is based off books known as Manga. Much like American tv, these shows fit into categories like Romantic Comedy, Drama, and Action. Nicholas's favorite type to watch is Rom-Com, as it is more close to the interactions he sees in real life.

Nicholas's Fantasy Football League

Since 2021, Nicholas has participated in Fantasy Football Leagues with his friends. For context, Fantasy Football is when u pick various offensive players from teams in the National Football League to score points based on statistics like touchdowns and passing yards. Switching gears, to add even more fuel to this fun, he and his friends will usually put money down before the season starts and payout the champion a good amount of money. This year came with a stipulation, however, as the money this year went to a jersey of the winner’s choice. There was also a limit of 140$ provided by the league, meaning the beneficiary had to pay the difference if his prize went above that. Speaking of that, the last thing of note here is that Nicholas won his first ever league in 2021.

Nicholas's Fascination with Fish

From a young age, Nicholas has had a deep fascination with fish. More specifically, he has tried to learn their various types. The one he is most intrigued with is the rainbow trout, though the Coi fish has caught his eye as well. While this isn't a very frequent hobby for him, he tries to learn as much information as he can when he has the time.
