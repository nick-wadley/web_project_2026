Nicholas's History With Sports

Growing up, Nicholas's two primary sports he participated in were basketball and track. Regarding the former, he was entrenched in that from the time he was in first grade all the way until his senior year of high school. As far as track goes, he did not begin that until high school.

  • Varsity Starting Center
  • Article Written About Him on a Prep Hoops Wesbite

Track Achievements

During his days as a track star, Nicholas also obtained many victories, including:

  • Varsity Lettermen 2021-2022

  • GHSA 110 Meter Hurdle State Championship Participant

Why Nicholas Fell In Love With Sports

There are multiple reasons for Nicholas falling in love with sports. First off, his mother was a walk-on basketball player during her time at William & Mary University, something that inspired him to pick up basketball. To continue along that point, his mom, along with his father, have both been sports fans since they were around Nick's current age. Furthermore, being from Metro Atlanta, Nicholas has grown up watching Atlanta Falcons and Hawks games. Therefore, it was seemingly destiny that he would fall in love with sports due to his early exposure.

Nicholas's Favorite NBA Player/Team

For a long time, Nicholas's favorite NBA player has been LeBron James. For context, James is considered one of the greatest players in NBA history, accruing accolades like being a 22x All-Star, 4x NBA Champion, and 4x Most Valuable Player. He has been Nicholas's favorite since he was eight years old, when he was a member of the Miami Heat. Since then, Nicholas has followed him from Miami, to the Cleveland Cavaliers, and now to the Lakers, who is his current favorite team. With LeBron seemingly at the end of his career now, Nick is debating on who he should root for moving forward.

Nicholas's Favorite NFL Team

Nicholas's favorite NFL team is the Seattle Seahawks, an American Football franchise based in Seattle, Washington. They have been his favorite since he was nine years old, something that has tested his loyalty due to their lack of playoff success over the past decade or so. Things have turned around, however, as his Seahawks won the Super Bowl this past Sunday over the New England Patriots, 29-13. With this resurgence, Nick looks forward to seeing how the team fairs next season.

Nicholas's Favorite Baseball Team

Nicholas's favorite baseball team is the Atlanta Braves, a pro baseball franchise based in Atlanta, Georgia. He has been exposed to them since birth, making it easy for him to support them. It also helps that most of his friends are Braves friends as well. Although they haven’t had any playoff success since winning the World Series in 2021, Nicholas has remained loyal to them. He hopes that things can turn around with a healthier roster, as injuries have plagued them the past couple of seasons.
